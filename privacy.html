Privacy and document access

ECG Learning Studio is an independent educational tool by Progress Ajayi. Public lessons and quizzes can be used without signing in.

Restricted document sign-in

When configured, Google verifies your email identity. The server uses read-only Google Drive metadata permission to check approval for the specific course folder and documents. It does not use that permission to read PDF contents. Google Drive serves and protects the originals in its viewer.

Sign-in tokens are held in an encrypted, Secure, HttpOnly session cookie for at most 12 hours. They are not placed in localStorage or public source code. Google and the hosting provider process requests under their own privacy policies. The app does not deliberately log tokens, document contents or private source links.

Local learning records

Notes, preferences, progress and practice activity are stored in your browser. Exported workspace files are under your control. Clearing site data removes local records and sign-in cookies. Signing out ends the app session, not your Google session.

Speech and the optional AI tutor

Read-aloud uses your browser’s speech service. Some voices run on your device; online voices may send the spoken text to their provider. Prefer a device voice for offline use. The app does not send private documents or study notes for narration.

Microphone recognition starts only after you accept the on-screen notice and press Start microphone. Your browser may send audio to its recognition provider. This app does not save recordings. You can correct the transcript before choosing a course command or sending a question.

When explicitly enabled by the owner, Ask AI tutor sends your question, up to six recent conversation turns and selected public teaching excerpts to OpenAI through a protected server endpoint. It does not retrieve private Drive documents, your study notes, email address or Google tokens for the AI prompt. Do not enter patient-identifiable information. The app holds the conversation only in page memory; Clear conversation or reloading removes it from the app. Requests use store:false; this does not override the provider’s applicable security, abuse-monitoring or retention policies. The initial AI trial is limited to the signed-in course owner and is disabled without separate configuration.

Approval and revocation

The administrator manages named-user viewing permission in Google Drive. Access is checked again when an original is requested. Previously viewed or downloaded material cannot be recalled by this app.
